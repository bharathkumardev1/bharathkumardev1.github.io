About

Hi, I'm Bharath.

I'm an AI/ML Engineer in Dallas, TX with 4+ years building ML systems, most recently LLM and retrieval systems. I work on search and question answering: finding the right passage in a huge pile of documents, and proving with a held-out test that a change made it better.

At Goldman Sachs I own the retrieval layer, the LangGraph agents and the evaluation for a production RAG platform that credit and risk analysts use. Before that I built a hospital-readmission risk model at Cigna Healthcare and demand forecasts for a retail client at Deloitte. I have an MS in Computer Science from the University of North Texas.

Finding the page that answers a question about a 10-K

Live demo

A search that finds the exact page of a 10-K that answers a question, across 360 SEC filings. The right page is in the top 10 results 71% of the time, up from 14% for plain keyword search. A heavier embedding version didn't beat it and ran about five times slower, so the simple one ships.

A research assistant that reviews its own answers

Design

Answers questions about research papers with cited passages, then reviews its own answer and searches again if it falls short. On 150 QASPER test questions it only ties a single pass over 5 passages and loses to a wider one, and the write-up says so.

Question→Retrieve top 5 passages→Answer cites [1] to [5]→Review, a second model call→Result↺refined query, up to 3 rounds
