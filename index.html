Retrieval over 2.68M Wikipedia passages

A LoRA-tuned MiniLM scores nDCG@10 0.402 on the full BEIR Natural Questions corpus, 0.014 above the untuned encoder (paired permutation p 0.0001) and 0.112 above my own BM25 (0.290). On a 104K-passage dev slice an INT8 ONNX cross-encoder over the top 20 adds 0.0941 nDCG@10 at 794 ms p50 on 2 vCPU. The hybrid and reranker were not run on the full corpus, and the README says so.

Forecasting taxi demand 45 minutes past the data

A LightGBM Poisson forecast of 15-minute pickups in 262 NYC zones: WAPE 0.2415 against 0.3104 for the best simple baseline. It loses in the two quietest zone quartiles. A Java Spring Boot gateway with a circuit breaker serves a static fallback (40 of 40 requests with the model killed), and a PSI drift monitor raised 36 false alarms in 1,776 clean-data checks.

A research assistant that reviews its own answers

A LangGraph loop that answers questions about research papers with cited passages. A second model call reviews each answer and asks for another search when it falls short, and every model reply is recorded so CI can replay the QASPER evaluation without an API key.

  • Scored every retrieval change on one 520-query benchmark kept apart from generation, with tuning on a separate dev set. Recall@10 rose from 84.6% to 91.7% over the dense FAISS baseline in four stages (metadata filtering +1.5 points, chunking and indexing +1.9, hybrid retrieval +2.2, a BGE cross-encoder over the top 30 +1.5), MRR from 0.73 to 0.84, and p95 retrieval latency fell from 1.42 s to 860 ms. RAGAS groundedness stayed above 90%. The stages are sequential differences, not isolated effects: no leave-one-out ablation was run.

Each completed step was validated and usable without rework; this is the share of steps automated, not time saved. Final credit judgments stay with analysts.
