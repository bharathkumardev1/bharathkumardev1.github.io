Finding the page that answers a question about a 10-K

FinanceBench labels the page that answers each of its 150 questions about 360 SEC filings, so retrieval can be measured on its own. Reading the company and period from the question and sending statement questions to statement pages takes Hit@10 from 0.14 to 0.75 on 100 held-out questions. An embedding hybrid tied BM25 at about five times the latency, so the service runs BM25: 126 queries a second on one core.

Who to call after a hospital discharge

The problem I worked on at Cigna, on 101,766 public encounters from 130 US hospitals: if a care team can call one discharged patient in ten, who should it be? A LightGBM call list reaches 25.0% of the patients readmitted within 30 days, against 20.5% when calling whoever had the most inpatient stays the year before. Every call comes with the three reasons behind its score.

Review until the answer is supported

A research assistant that reviews its own answers

A LangGraph loop that answers questions about research papers and cites the passages it used. A second model call reviews each answer against those passages and asks for another search when it falls short. I'm measuring whether that review earns its extra calls over a single pass on QASPER, and every model reply is recorded so CI can rerun the evaluation without an API key.

The QASPER comparison isn't published yet. The loop, the evaluation harness and the replay are in the repo now.
